About

Evaluations past human language.

I am an undergraduate in Mathematics and Computing at Georgia Tech, with a concentration in Intelligence.

I want to build evaluations for superintelligence that goes beyond human language, so safety does not rest on one kind of test. The measures are mechanistic interpretability, and evaluations made of complicated tasks and scenarios. Recent work, with Professor Wei Xu, is on how language models handle emerging language. I am glad to talk.
