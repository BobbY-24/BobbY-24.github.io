About

Optimization, language, and a measurement you can defend.

I am an undergraduate in Industrial and Systems Engineering at Georgia Tech, with a Computer Science minor on the Intelligence thread.

The through-line is using statistical rigor and algorithmic thinking on problems that do not sit still: operations, language, and systems where efficiency, fairness, and automation are all in play. Recent work is in natural language processing, especially emerging language and the evaluation of large language models. I am looking toward machine learning engineering, AI research, and applied data science, and I am glad to talk.
